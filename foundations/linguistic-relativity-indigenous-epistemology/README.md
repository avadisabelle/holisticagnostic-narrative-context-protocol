## Purpose

This packet provides the academic grounding for two interlocking processes and their implications for the Narrative Context Protocol:

1. **How language pre-structures what can be said** — linguistic relativity, ethnolinguistics, and the grammatical encoding of worldview
2. **How knowledge earns the right to be told** — Indigenous epistemology, storywork methodology, and the validation workflows that govern narrative transmission

Together, these fields reveal that the NCP currently models *what a story contains* but not *the conditions under which a story may travel*. This packet proposes decolonizing extensions to address that gap.

## Central Insight

> A story is not a document. It is an act with conditions. Oral traditions already ARE protocols — they just aren't written in JSON.
